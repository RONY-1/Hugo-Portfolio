### Project Overview
The Smart Medical System Software Application provides a unified desktop and database framework for clinics and diagnostic centers to manage patient health records securely.

### Key Capabilities
- Digital patient history and prescription generator.
- Appointment scheduling and doctor duty roster.
- Patient database search and structured analytics.
