### Overview
MedVision-XAI Studio is an interactive dashboard designed for medical researchers to inspect deep neural network predictions on radiological images.

### Key Capabilities
- Real-time Grad-CAM and LayerCAM heatmap generation.
- Side-by-side DICOM image comparison.
- Exportable clinical interpretation summary reports.
