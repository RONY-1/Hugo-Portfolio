### Abstract
Early detection of Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) is crucial for preventing vision impairment. In this paper, we propose a vision transformer (ViT) architecture coupled with Grad-CAM++ feature visualization to deliver interpretable, high-accuracy lesion localization on high-resolution fundus images.

### Key Contributions
- Achieved **96.4% diagnostic sensitivity** across standard benchmark datasets.
- Developed an open-source PyTorch interpretation package for clinical heatmaps.
- Validated model explanations with certified ophthalmologists.
