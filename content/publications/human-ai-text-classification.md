### Overview
This conference paper investigates natural language processing techniques and feature representations to discriminate human-written text from modern Large Language Model (LLM) generated outputs.

### Key Contributions
- Formulated NLP evaluation benchmark for synthetic text detection.
- Evaluated classifier performance across multiple dataset domains.
- Published at 1st International Conference proceedings.
