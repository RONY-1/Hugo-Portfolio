Hybrid Explainable Models for Aviation Security: Detecting ADS-B Injection Attacks with XAI Techniques

June 21, 2024 · 1 min · 35 words · Md. Rony
Table of Contents
    • Overview

Overview#

This research paper addresses signal spoofing threats in automatic dependent surveillance-broadcast (ADS-B) flight tracking. We introduce a hybrid XAI model that detects injection anomalies and provides interpretable feature risk scores for air traffic management.
